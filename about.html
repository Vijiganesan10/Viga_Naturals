Since Day One

Crafted With Roots in Tradition

Viga Naturals began as a small kitchen experiment — reviving the herbal hair oils and bathing powders our grandmothers used, at a time when store shelves were full of ingredient lists no one could pronounce.

Every formula is developed in small batches, tested patiently, and made only with ingredients we'd be comfortable explaining to a five-year-old — real herbs, cold-pressed oils and time-honoured Ayurvedic recipes.

Today, thousands of customers trust Viga Naturals for hair care, skin care and body care that's honest, effective and genuinely natural.
